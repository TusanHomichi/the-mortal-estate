## Explicit provisional choices

Starting health, mana, stamina, nationality, equipment and skill levels remain
authored integration defaults. Weapon users start with a staff; the Martial
Artist starts with an empty hand. Casters receive a book bound through the normal
creation owner. These loadouts are not asserted to reproduce the historical
class-specific equipment table. Attribute allocation currently does not
recalculate resources or combat ratings.

Combat ratings, the cellar encounter, its loot and experience, level thresholds,
class growth distributions, regeneration, merchant stock and prices, bank limits,
locker capacity and all training curves are provisional. Class growth currently
shares the inherited integration distribution. Initial item, spell and numeric
rule definitions derive from the project's original adventure integration
fixture. The unrelated trail quest and sentinel are excluded.

The testable loop uses the current prepaid-learning-rate implementation; its
remaining difference from the recovered five-rank account remains in the class
contract. An instructor's supported tracks are deliberately bounded to this
expedition's item and spell selection; the complete skill and spell catalogs are
later content, not silently absent historical features.

Town residents stand at reachable service squares in front of the reviewed
furniture. Their positions are gameplay cast placement and do not alter the
accepted floor masks. The presentation study instantiates only server-observed
residents at those positions. The lodge recovery table remains a visual feature;
ordinary ground item transfer is available, but a separately owned table-storage
mechanic has not been added. The sheriff is a resident under the carried social
rules; dedicated patrol and arrest behavior is not implemented by this seed.
The authored members currently retain the unclassified spell-law zone. The
bounded spell selection has no terrain-alignment violation; town-specific
terrain-magic enforcement must be authored and proved before that selection
expands. The expedition execution record owns this open content finding.

The geographic receipt grants no gameplay-tuning authority. Numeric reconciliation
belongs to the gameplay baseline and must retain these unresolved facts until
replaced by supported behavior and proof.
