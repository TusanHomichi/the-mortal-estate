# Death And Corpse Fixture Provenance

`death_corpse.json` is an original clean project fixture for Slice DK.

- The room, coordinates, actors, items, statistics, gold, and deterministic
  script are newly authored for this project.
- Generic fantasy words such as corpse, ghost, knife, club, and brute are used
  descriptively and do not identify or copy a source-game row.
- The fixture does not copy a private corpse catalog entry, creature mapping,
  map cell, drop table, numeric value, prose passage, art, or audio asset.
- `corpse:1` and `corpse:2` are project's deterministic generated test IDs. They
  exercise the typed command contract and are not source data.
- Retained private evidence informed only the normalized qualitative boundary
  recorded in the committed Slice DK design: hands drop, other carried
  property and gold may remain with an independent corpse, same-hex search
  releases contents, and non-fire player defeat leaves a ghost and corpse.
