# Gold Bank And Locker Storage Fixture Provenance

This Simulation Scenario graph is original project clean content. Its room
layout, actor and service names, stable bank and locker-vault IDs, item
definition, transaction cap, locker capacity, gold amounts, and scripted order
were authored specifically for deterministic Slice ED verification.

Reviewed evidence informed only the normalized qualitative behaviors: gold can
occupy carried positions or ground piles, bank branches may share one
character-keyed balance, and locker access points may share one
character-keyed vault. The fixture does not reproduce target world
coordinates, dialogue, art, item rows, or numeric tuning. Item offers remain
covered by focused rules/contract tests because this clean fixture has one
player character.

The fixture must remain independent of quarantined private-reference and
runtime-placeholder material and is validated through the ordinary
clean-content path.
