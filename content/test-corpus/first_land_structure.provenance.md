# First Land Structure Provenance

The tracked First Land graph is clean, original project content. Private
predecessor references informed only broad landmark relationships and
travel rhythm. No
source coordinate, terrain row, topology payload, tracing, transformed
geometry, art, or recovered gameplay payload entered tracked content.

The predecessor authored this graph with an offline blueprint compiler.
Neither that compiler nor its authored master is carried here; the graph
arrives as migrated test data and is maintained as such. Runtime never
loads an authoring format.

- Catalog 6 owns 86 terrains and the other immutable definitions.
- World Template 3 owns six members, 19,156 cells, 8,977 passable cells, the
  south-dock arrival, and 197 topology rows.
- Simulation Seed 3 owns seven actors, services, ground state, 38 ecology
  sites, and 92 starting slots.
- The four dungeon members are geometrically preserved. Temple and overgrown
  stairs are reciprocal; the forge-route pit is one-way to `lake_level`; two
  portal landmarks remain inert.

The World Template, seed, and catalog rows in this corpus were generated,
not hand-authored. They are edited here only as a unit and only with the
golden transcripts regenerated, because the graph's connectivity,
placements, and dungeon geometry are load-bearing for the simulation
harness.
