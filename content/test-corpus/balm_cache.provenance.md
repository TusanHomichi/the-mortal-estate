# Balm Cache Fixture Provenance

This fixture is clean, original project-authored content. It exists to exercise
balm-style consumable healing: a damaged player drinks a carried balm
mid-combat, the healing effect ticks each round and races the monster's
attacks, the final tick caps at max hp and ends the effect, and a second balm
drunk at full hp is consumed with no healing.

It does not copy private maps, coordinates, source text, source names, packet
schemas, numeric tables, or old-client data.
