# Spell Effects Fixture Provenance

This fixture is clean, original project-authored content. It exists to exercise
direct damage, self-healing, spell-sourced active effects, warmed casting,
and one intentionally stubbed spell in a single reviewable script.

It uses generic RPG labels and does not copy private source text, private
packet content, old-client data, or normalized research payloads.
