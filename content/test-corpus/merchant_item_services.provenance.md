# Merchant Item Services Provenance

`merchant_item_services.json` is clean original project content. Its provider
names, stock, item names, prices, pawn multiplier, service costs, enchantment,
map, and scripted sequence were authored only to prove the generic Slice EC
architecture.

The fixture does not claim commercial stock, placement, pricing, provider,
restock, repair, or durability parity. Exact target constants remain confined
to the marked economy parity pack and its validator; the open stock and
provider questions remain open there.
