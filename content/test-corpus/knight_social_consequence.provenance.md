`knight_social_consequence.json` is original clean project content created for
the Slice EG town-law and delegated class-consequence proof.

- The court, actor and spell names, coordinates, terrain, item instances,
  combat values, spell values, and scripted order were authored from scratch.
- Reviewed normalized target evidence informed only the qualitative seams
  exercised here: a town terrain-law alignment change, lawful-animal handling,
  an unjust lawful-human consequence, karma mutation, and current-Knight class
  loss through the existing promotion owner.
- The starting Knight sheet, ring instance, and combat/resource numbers are
  deterministic proof inputs assembled from current clean contracts; they do
  not claim exact target placement, provider identity, replacement policy, or
  full Knighthood lifecycle parity.
- No private map, dialogue, named source character, account state, server
  behavior, art, or unresolved formula was copied into clean content.

Private evidence remains quarantined. Exact normalized claims and their open
or server-deferred boundaries remain in approved marked parity data rather
than in this clean fixture.
