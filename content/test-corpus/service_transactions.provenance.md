# Service Transactions Fixture Provenance

This Simulation Scenario graph is an original clean project architecture proof.
The provider, transaction, item names, positions, quantities, gold cost, and
XP reward were authored for this repository. They are not copied, transformed,
or numerically inferred from commercial game data, ignored research, or the
marked parity pack.

The fixture proves typed discovery, exact carried-item selection, partial
stack consumption, carried-gold cost, experience reward, fixed item grant,
and the final committed transaction event. It is not evidence of commercial
service-row parity.
