# Resting Hollow Fixture Provenance

This fixture is clean, original project-authored content. It exists to exercise
passive out-of-combat player regeneration: a damaged player fails to regenerate
while adjacent to a living hostile, then regains hp on deterministic cadence
rounds after retreating beyond adjacency, capped at max hp.

It does not copy private maps, coordinates, source text, source names, packet
schemas, numeric tables, or old-client data.
