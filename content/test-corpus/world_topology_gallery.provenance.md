# World Topology Gallery Provenance

`world_topology_gallery.json` is clean project-authored Slice EK prototype
content.

The realm names, level names, layered cells, arrivals, topology, character,
spell placement, route, and numeric tuning are original deterministic fixture
material. The gallery demonstrates the generic Catalog 2, World Template 2,
Simulation Seed 2, and unified navigation contracts.

The fixture makes no claim about commercial terrain identity, terrain cost,
layer rendering, water formulas, route geometry, arrival coordinates, or
actor, item, service, creature, quest, shop, or spawn placement.
