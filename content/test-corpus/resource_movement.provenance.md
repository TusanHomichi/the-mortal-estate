# Resource Movement Gallery Provenance

`resource_movement.json` is clean project-authored prototype content for Slice
DJ.

The fixture exercises the normalized qualitative categories for one-, two-,
and three-direction movement, four burden tiers, movement exertion, actor-local
activity, and ordered HP/MP/stamina recovery. Its deliberately small burden
thresholds make boundary assertions readable; they are test tuning, not a
recovered source formula.

The practice hall, actor and item names, map, coordinates, item rows, numeric
tuning, resources, and script sequence are original project test values. They do
not copy private geography, source coordinates, item catalogs, exact formulas,
packet schemas, or quarantined local-reference payloads.
